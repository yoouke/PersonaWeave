# Third-party components

`personaweave/vendor/astrbot_api.py` reuses the `Api` helper from
`Jack-Cherish/DSQQ/vendor/dafeiyu-config-pack/tools/import_dafeiyu.py`.
The pack identifies upstream `zexuanw958-svg/dafeiyu-config-pack`, revision `8c01b03`.
The selected six AstrBot plugin identifiers follow that pack's `settings/pack.json`.
No fixed persona, private data, voice samples or full DSQQ installer source is included.
